Program Overview

This guide supports facilitators delivering the WISE Financial Literacy program. Use it alongside the lesson templates and supporting materials to plan sessions, prepare activities, and align outcomes.

Learning Objectives

  • Build foundational money management skills.
  • Strengthen budgeting and saving habits.
  • Introduce responsible credit and debt practices.
  • Encourage goal setting and financial planning.
